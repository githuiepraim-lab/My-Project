JARVIS Remote

Put JARVIS on your phone's home screen. It opens full-screen like an app, with its own icon.

iPhone / iPad your device

  1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open JARVIS from your home screen and enter the key shown on your computer.

Android your device

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open JARVIS from your home screen and enter the key shown on your computer.

Good to know

  • Your phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi. The computer must be on and JARVIS running.
  • This page only installs the app shell. Nothing private is stored by it: logging in still needs the one-time key from your computer.
  • If your browser warns about the certificate, that is the computer's own self-made one; continue once and then install.

Away from home or computer off?

Use the Telegram connection instead: it works anywhere, on iPhone and Android, with the official Telegram app. Set it up in JARVIS under ✈ TELEGRAM.
